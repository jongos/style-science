# Write for the Reader

Apply this pass to copy Dazzler creates or is authorized to edit: page introductions, UI labels, reports, slides, captions, callouts and supporting prose. Infer audience and purpose from the brief. A visual-only change does not authorize rewriting supplied copy. Preserve approved wording, quotations, legal language, data, citations, code and localization keys.

Resolve conflicts in this order: user intent and protected facts; accessibility and task usability; the chosen design direction; editorial preferences. Good design takes priority over copy-pattern rules. Keep a short label, punchy fragment, repeated phrase or stylized heading when it improves navigation, composition or voice. A writing preference must not erase useful hierarchy or break a working layout.

## Preserve Meaning and Voice

Read the surrounding material before editing. Keep the author's useful vocabulary, humor, level of formality, uncertainty and sentence rhythm. Make the smallest change that improves clarity. A distinctive fragment or longer sentence can stay; do not make every paragraph follow the same pattern.

Prefer direct verbs, understandable terms and a clear subject. Keep precise technical language when the audience needs it. Preserve qualifications and uncertainty: changing “may reduce delays” to “reduces delays” changes the claim. Never add metrics, endorsements, opinions or examples merely to make copy sound specific.

Test generic copy by asking whether it could describe an unrelated product unchanged. Replace it with an available fact, mechanism, reader action or consequence. If the evidence is missing, narrow the claim or flag the gap; do not invent support. Keep stable names for the same product, action or concept across headings, navigation, charts and body copy.

## Review Patterns in Context

| Pattern | Useful revision |
| --- | --- |
| Delayed point or manufactured revelation | Remove the setup when it adds no context; keep a meaningful story or personal aside |
| Empty praise or unsupported authority | State supported behavior or name the actual source; flag consequential claims that cannot be verified |
| Repeated contrast formulas or dramatic fragments | State the concrete point directly unless the contrast or cadence serves the content |
| Commentary announcing significance | Supply the relevant consequence, or remove commentary when the evidence already makes the point |
| Vague verbs and interchangeable nouns | Name the action and object; keep terminology consistent rather than cycling synonyms |
| Repeated sentence and paragraph shapes | Adjust structure where it impedes reading, without forcing artificial variety |
| Inflated conclusion or redundant recap | End on the useful conclusion, unresolved decision or next action; keep an executive summary when the format needs it |

These are editing candidates, not word bans or evidence of AI authorship. Preserve meaningful contrast, uncertainty, conventional labels, literal domain terms, deliberate satire and the user's requested voice. Use punctuation for clarity; do not enforce arbitrary dash or colon quotas.

## Keep the Design Expressive

Editorial clarity complements [document design](document-design.md). Retain purposeful bold and colored keywords, italics, callouts, section backgrounds, lists and quotations. Remove emphasis that obscures hierarchy, not emphasis itself. Do not flatten a designed document into plain prose or remove required navigation and summaries. English headings retain Dazzler's protected Title Case; body sentences follow their language and grammar.

For interfaces, name what a control does, keep terminology consistent with its destination, and give errors a supported next step. Never invent a recovery action the product cannot perform. Keep accessible names aligned with visible labels and review wrapping after copy changes.

## Final Editorial Pass

Compare the revision with the source: facts, quantities, qualifications, citations, intent, voice and protected text must survive. Check that new claims have support, repetition has a purpose and the design still fits the brief. Then rerun affected heading and layout checks under [final artifact gates](delivery-gates.md).

For an explicit critique or detection-only request, report the specific passage, observed pattern and a brief suggested correction; do not rewrite the artifact, assign an AI probability or claim who wrote it. In normal creation/editing, apply the pass automatically and summarize only material changes. Do not append an editorial report to every designed artifact.
